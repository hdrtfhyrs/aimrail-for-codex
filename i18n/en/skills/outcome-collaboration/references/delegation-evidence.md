# Evidence and boundaries for delegation decisions

Expand these originals as needed when division is disputed. They inform tradeoffs rather than fixed steps. Findings recorded on 2026-10-02:

- [Anthropic's research system](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/multi-agent-research-system) suits multiple independent research directions. Vague delegation can duplicate or omit investigation, and excessive agents/updates can interfere. Programming tasks with substantial shared context/dependencies may not suit the same method. Vendor self-evaluated gains and token ratios cannot become general local conclusions.
- [Official Claude team documentation](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/agent-teams) supports independent file ownership and task dependencies. Strong sequencing or same-file work raises coordination costs. Its example headcounts, periodic monitoring, and optional quality hooks are not local requirements; local important reports and the user's current authorization govern. The documentation still called teams experimental at the recorded reading; reading it did not authorize configuration enablement.
- [OpenAI subagent documentation](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/agent-configuration/subagents) describes independent context moving exploration logs out of the main thread, conflict handling for parallel writes, and extra model/tool overhead. The main thread retains goals and decisions; the responsible executor directly writes formal outcomes to files.
- The Chinese-authored [MetaGPT Chinese tutorial](https://docs.deepwisdom.ai/main/zh/guide/tutorials/multi_agent_101.html) uses important upstream artifacts to trigger later actions. Its [role source code](https://github.com/FoundationAgents/MetaGPT/blob/11cdf466d042aece04fc6cfd13b28e1a70341b1f/metagpt/roles/role.py#L399) filters new messages by watched sources/recipients. Adopt the idea of transmitting only relevant dependencies, without importing company roles, round-by-round SOPs, or rebuilding a messaging platform.
- [MASFT research v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.13657v1) found role violations, failure to share important information, ignoring peers' views, and incorrect termination in its then-current frameworks, models, and task samples. Prompt/topology improvements did not solve all issues. This supports looking at actual final outcomes, without providing an optimal current-model headcount or permanent reliability guarantee.

The local headcount/AI-consumer misjudgment case (`workspace/memory/experiences/goal-context-before-actions.md`) preserves original evidence of 19 creations, an old fixed headcount of 6, and a diagram wrongly delegated as a webpage; long-term fixes remain unverified. “Rule bloat” (`workspace/memory/experiences/rule-and-workflow-bloat.md`) establishes only local reduction in burden/delivery. Using these boundaries, the recorded round divided ownership into three independent outcomes—accounts, files, and collaboration—with the file owner integrating navigation. This was an arrangement for those input/write conditions, not a headcount template.

Detailed evidence, actual adoption, and unmet items for that round are in the delegation improvement record (`modules/system/成果/本轮账号与文件整合/分工改进登记/分工改进与试用说明.md`). Historical source locations are retained for traceability; deployment-specific case records or outcomes may be absent from the sanitized public release and are not required to run the shared code.
