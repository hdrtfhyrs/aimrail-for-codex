# Problem judgment and resolution

Historical Pi instructions: current user conditions and host rules take precedence. See the [Pi guide](../../README.md).

Distinguish goals, methods, examples, and authoritative sources. The user's goals and preferences are authoritative, but both their proposed technical method and the assistant's plan require investigation. Repetition does not establish understanding. On project takeover, inspect the purpose, relevant historical statements, and later corrections. Read general errors by relevant category rather than loading the whole library. For new proposals, unfamiliar problems, or unknowns that change the route, use [adaptive-research](../adaptive-research/SKILL.md) to investigate mature practice, failures, and alternatives. Explain the actual mechanism, deliverable, effect, and cost to the user, then implement under existing authorization. New feedback corrects affected steps; wait for the user only for genuinely missing key information that changes the route. Arrange independent challenge for important judgments according to their value and actual tools/permissions. Simple execution within an established, verified route need not repeat the whole process, but a small task does not justify skipping evidence that affects understanding of its goal.

Prefer mature solutions. Recommend changing route when evidence does not support the method rather than following the user blindly or defending the assistant's prior proposal. Research relevant domestic/international official and community sources, excluding news in this historical skill. Reuse the research skill's evidence and stopping rules instead of creating another process. Separate quality judgment from process cost. Stop when the smallest direct evidence establishes that the real goal is met and remaining uncertainty is acceptable; do not add complete testing or publication as new goals. Follow the user's explicit verification requests.

When blocked, distinguish missing information, paths, tools, permissions, and design. Supplement inputs, switch routes, split the work, or assign a suitable executor instead of retrying blindly. Abstract stable judgment principles without elevating a novel-writing prohibition or one correction to global policy. Read evidence or collaboration skills only when the current issue needs their methods; do not expand all skills for simple execution.
